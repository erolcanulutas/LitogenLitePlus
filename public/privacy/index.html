Privacy

How Litogen handles your information. Last updated 31 August 2026.

Your pictures never leave your computer

Litogen turns a picture into a 3D model entirely inside your browser. The picture is read, measured and converted on your own machine, and the file you download is built there too. It is never uploaded to us, and we never see it.

What we keep, and only if you make an account

You can use Litogen without an account. If you make one, we store:

  • your email address;
  • either a scrambled form of your password — never the password itself — or, if you signed in with Google, the identifier Google gives us for your account;
  • the date the account was made.

That is all. We do not ask for your name, your address or anything else.

Signing in with Google

If you use the Google button, Google tells us your email address and an identifier for your account, and confirms the address belongs to you. We ask Google for nothing else — no contacts, no files, no profile beyond that. Google never gives us your password.

Cookies

One cookie, so that you stay signed in between visits. There is no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking on this site.

Failed sign-ins

When a sign-in fails we record the address it came from for one day, so that passwords cannot be guessed at machine speed. These records delete themselves.

Where it is kept, and who else sees it

Account records sit on the server that runs litogen.app. Traffic to the site passes through Cloudflare, which delivers the pages and, in doing so, sees the network address of every visitor — as any provider carrying the traffic would.

Deleting your account

Write to erolcan@litogen.app and we will remove it and everything attached to it.

Changes

If this ever changes materially, the date at the top changes with it.

Questions about any of this: erolcan@litogen.app
